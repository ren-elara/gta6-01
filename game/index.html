This game needs a browser with Canvas and JavaScript enabled.

Enter starts or restarts. WASD or arrows move and drive. Shift sprints. E or F enters or exits a car. Mouse aims; click or Space fires. H opens the story briefing. C opens the paused courier exchange: 1–4, up/down arrows, or clicking a route selects it; Enter or the accept button starts it; X or Cancel cancels it without losing the story. G collects or delivers at a violet depot within 100 metres at low speed. Gold marks the story; violet marks a contract. P or Escape pauses. L saves, O loads, M toggles sound, and R toggles reduced motion. Courier deadlines count playing time only. C also shows records after chapter completion.
